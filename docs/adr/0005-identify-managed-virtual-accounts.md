# Identify managed virtual accounts

Mobile displays the shared database's permanent virtual-account classification on profiles, posts and comments and keeps synthetic engagement separate from organic counts. Managed identities cannot sign in or receive contact actions; users may hide virtual posts. The originating database decision and deployment gate are recorded in `yrdly-app/docs/adr/0002-isolate-managed-virtual-engagement.md` and `yrdly-app/docs/managed-virtual-accounts.md`.
