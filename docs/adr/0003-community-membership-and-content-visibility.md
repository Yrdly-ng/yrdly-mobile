# Separate community discovery from member content access

Open and request communities expose descriptive metadata to nonmembers, while community posts require active membership. Invite-only communities require an invitation, and invite recipients may inspect enough metadata to accept or decline without gaining access to posts. This gives up public post previews in exchange for a consistent membership privacy boundary across mobile, web, and Supabase.

Location communities may initially enrol residents automatically, but a voluntary departure or ban must survive profile changes. Location convenience does not grant automatic authority to restore a membership the person or community ended.

Interest communities belong to their creator upon approval; official ward/LGA communities remain under YRDLY staff oversight. An interest community's owner alone appoints admins, admins appoint moderators, and ownership transfers require acceptance. Invitations are restricted to mutual friends who have not blocked one another, and shared community links never bypass admission rules. These constraints prioritise consent and stable community authority over unrestricted growth.
