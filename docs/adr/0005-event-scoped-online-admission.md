# Event-scoped online admission

Assigned event duties and invitations are separate from owned events and guest tickets. Staff use their own accounts and the authenticated event team API; every admission is checked online and no cached role authorises entry. Financial and organiser settings remain outside staff access. See the product app [decision](../../../yrdly-app/docs/adr/0002-event-scoped-online-admission.md) for the database permission and concurrency rules.
