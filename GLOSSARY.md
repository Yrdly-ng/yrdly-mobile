# YRDLY

The language of YRDLY's neighbourhood network, communities, and account activity.

## Language

**Activity notification**:
A saved update in an account's Notifications screen. It remains available when the account has disabled device alerts for its category.

**Device alert**:
A notification presented on a device where the recipient is signed in. Its preview can summarize grouped activity and omits private message content, payment amounts, and dispute details.

**Notification group**:
Related activity for one recipient within the same conversation or post, summarized together in a device alert with the latest update and a count.

### Communities

**Community**:
A group centred on a neighbourhood or shared interest whose posts are visible to its members.

**Open community**:
A community people can join immediately. Nonmembers may see its description, but not its posts.

**Request community**:
A community whose membership requires approval of a join request. Nonmembers may see its description, but not its posts.

**Invite-only community**:
A community whose membership requires an invitation. An invite recipient may see enough information to accept or decline.

**Location community**:
A community associated with a ward or local government area. Initial enrolment follows a person's home area, while voluntary departure and bans remain respected.

**Community owner**:
The person responsible for an interest community, entitled to appoint admins and transfer ownership before leaving.

**Community admin**:
A community leader who manages settings, ordinary members, and moderators.

**Community moderator**:
A member entrusted with content review, join requests, invitations, and bans, without authority over owners or admins.

**Join request**:
A person's application to become a member of a request community.

**Community invitation**:
A revocable offer of membership from a community leader to a mutual friend, which its recipient may accept or decline within seven days.

**Community ban**:
A restriction preventing a person from joining or participating in a community until lifted.

**Pending community content**:
A post or reply awaiting review before publication, with its review status visible to its author.

**Ownership transfer**:
An offer to hand responsibility for an interest community to another person, effective only after the recipient accepts.

**Community announcement**:
A notice published by an owner or admin for the community's active members.

**Pending content revision**:
An edited version of a published community post or reply awaiting approval while the previously published version remains visible.
