# YRDLY Notifications

Updates about activity relevant to a YRDLY account and the device alerts that draw attention to them.

## Language

**Activity notification**:
A saved update in an account's Notifications screen. It remains available when the account has disabled device alerts for its category.

**Device alert**:
A notification presented on a device where the recipient is signed in. Its preview can summarize grouped activity and omits private message content, payment amounts, and dispute details.

**Notification group**:
Related activity for one recipient within the same conversation or post, summarized together in a device alert with the latest update and a count.
